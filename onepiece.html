What OnePiece does

OnePiece keeps track of your NSE/BSE holdings, imported broker trades, SIP dip levels, and live CMP tracking in one clean dashboard.

Built for Indian stocks

The app is tuned for Moneycontrol-style exports and Indian market symbols, so imports and codes line up with the way you already track trades.

Live view

Once signed in, holdings update row by row as quotes arrive, with portfolio value, P&L, and XIRR recalculated on the fly.

Quick start

  • Sign in or create an account
  • Import your Excel trade history
  • Review the portfolio and SIP pages

1. Import trades

Upload your broker statement and let the importer parse buys and sells into your trade history automatically.

2. Resolve symbols

We resolve stock labels into exchange codes so the portfolio can fetch prices and show the correct company name.

3. Track performance

Portfolio rows show CMP, change, total value, P&L, and XIRR so you can see what changed without opening another sheet.

4. Plan SIP dips

The SIP page turns falling prices into allocation triggers, helping you decide when to deploy capital in steps.

Do I need login to browse?

No. These public pages are available immediately. Login is only required for your personal data, trades, portfolio, and settings.

What stays private?

Your holdings, imported trades, watchlist, and SIP settings are still protected behind authentication.

Can I see the app without an account?

Yes. The public overview shows the product concept and workflow so you can decide if you want to sign in.

Need help?

Use the sign in area below to create an account, or come back after you're ready to import your trade files.

Security boundaries

The public pages are read-only. Anything that changes personal data still requires a valid session token. That keeps sign-in flows, trade import, and portfolio edits separate from the public landing content.
